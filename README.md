## Featured projects

### [CIMNE International](https://github.com/jquirosv/CimneInternational-Portfolio)

A full-stack web platform for an arts education institution, combining a public website with academic management and artist and event workflows.

- Responsive institutional website and program pages.
- Student records, enrollment, scheduling, attendance, makeup lessons, and academic cycles.
- Tuition records, teacher settlements, repertoire, concerts, and reporting.
- Role and module permissions, email campaigns, and internal conversations.
- Artist and event management through CIMNE Live, and the CIMNE Productions catalog.

**Built with:** JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, and MySQL.

### [Nimbo](https://github.com/jquirosv/Nimbo-Portfolio)

A native Android music player combining a local library, personalized playback, and listening insights.

- Phone and SD music library, playlists, and background playback.
- Themes, typography, artwork palettes, and assisted lyrics lookup.
- Account-separated statistics and daily statistical backups.
- Monthly stories exported as images or video, with an independent soundtrack.

**Built with:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Media3, WorkManager, SQLite, Python, Flask, and MySQL.

### [ChordStream](https://github.com/jquirosv/ChordStream-Portfolio)

An Android songbook for musicians to organize, edit, and perform songs, with a local library and collaborative group features.

- Song editor with chord-aligned lyrics, tablatures, and transposition.
- Setlists, auto-scroll, and instrument-specific views.
- Local SQLite storage, backup import and export, and ChordPro support.
- Accounts, groups, and synchronization through a PHP/MySQL API.

**Built with:** Dart, Flutter, Provider, SQLite, PHP, and MySQL.
